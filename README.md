## Objective

Estimate the prevalence of artemisinin-resistance (kelch13) and partner-drug or antifolate markers (crt, mdr1, dhfr) in *P. falciparum* from Benin using whole-genome sequencing data from ENA, classifying each sample as wild-type, mixed or mutant at every marker codon.

## Methods

- Reads capped to 5 million pairs per sample (seqtk, seed 42), mapped with bwa mem to the MalariaGEN Pf7 3D7 reference, duplicates marked (GATK MarkDuplicates); loci BAMs hold reads overlapping each gene +/- 2 kb. This stage ran in an earlier session; this notebook restores its saved output.
- HaplotypeCaller (haploid, gVCF) on the four loci; CombineGVCFs and GenotypeGVCFs; hard filters (QD < 2, FS > 60, SOR > 3, MQ < 40); SnpEff database Plasmodium_falciparum (contigs renamed 1-14 and back).
- Marker calls come from read counts at the whole codon: reads with MAPQ >= 20, base quality >= 20 at the three bases, not duplicates, one count per read pair. no_call if fewer than 10 reads; mutant if at least 80% of reads carry the mutant amino acid; mixed if at least 3 mutant reads and at least 10%; otherwise wild-type. Prevalence = samples with a mutant or mixed call among called samples, Wilson 95% confidence interval.
- Versions: GATK: The Genome Analysis Toolkit (GATK) v4.6.2.0; samtools: samtools 1.24; bcftools: bcftools 1.24; SnpEff: SnpEff	5.4c	2026-02-23

## Interpretation

Prevalence is the share of called samples with a mutant or mixed call (Wilson 95% CI). With 8 samples from one
study (collected 2014 and 2016), the intervals are wide and the results describe these parasites only.

**Artemisinin (kelch13).** No WHO-validated propeller marker was detected: C580Y 0/8 (CI 0-32%), A675V 0/6
(0-39%), and no mutant or mixed call at any other panel marker in the samples where it was covered (the
number of called samples per marker is in results/marker_prevalence.tsv). R561H was callable in only 2
samples and is uninformative. Joint calling found two kelch13 coding changes, neither a panel marker:
K189T (5 of 7 genotyped), a common African polymorphism not considered a resistance marker as far as I
know, and R255K (1 of 7), outside the propeller; its classification should be checked in the current WHO
list. A true prevalence of up to about 32% cannot be excluded.

**Chloroquine (crt).** All samples with spanning reads carry the CVIET haplotype at codons 72-76 (7 of 7;
ERR15630284: 32 CVIET reads and 1 CVMNK read), K76T was mutant in 5 of 5 called samples (CI 57-100%), and
the joint call finds K76T in 8 of 8. The two adjacent crt "frameshift" calls (403618 A>AT and 403622 AT>A,
each in 8 of 8 samples) are the representation of M74I and N75E by the caller (net length change zero:
ATGAATAAA becomes ATTGAAAAA, and ATTGAAACA with K76T); read counts confirm M74I 7/7 and N75E 6/6. Secondary
crt changes usually found with K76T are present (A220S 7/7, Q271E 8/8, R371I 6/8, I356T 4/8).

**Partner drug (mdr1).** N86Y was mutant in 3 of 7 called samples (43%, CI 16-75%) and Y184F in 6 of 7
(86%, CI 49-97%), in agreement with the joint call (N86Y 3/8, Y184F 6/7). No mutant or mixed call at S1034C,
N1042D or D1246Y where covered. N86Y is the marker with an intermediate frequency; its interval is wide.

**Antifolate (dhfr).** N51I 8/8 (CI 68-100%), S108N 7/7 (65-100%) and C59R 3/3 (44-100%; callable in only 3
samples), with all three in 8 of 8 samples by joint call: the triple mutant is present in every sample.
I164L was absent (0/8, CI 0-32%). dhps, the other SP target, was not analysed.

**Data quality.** ERR15620828 (median codon depth 5.5) and ERR15630236 (median 9.0; 53.8% of reads mapped)
have weak coverage. Codons R561 and C59 are covered in the 2016 samples (150 bp reads) but not in the 2014
samples (100 bp reads); the cause was not investigated. The joint call can count a genotype supported by very
few reads, so read-count results are the well-supported ones.


## Limitations

- 8 samples from one study give wide confidence intervals: 0 of 8 is compatible with a true prevalence up to about 32%.
- Reads were capped at 5 million pairs per sample; minor clones below about 10% of reads are not detectable.
- A haploid genotype call hides mixed infections; the marker calls above use read counts instead.
- Samples with weak coverage (median codon depth below 20): ERR15620828, ERR15630236.
- Markers called in fewer than 5 samples (interpret with caution): kelch13 P553L (n=2), kelch13 R561H (n=2), dhfr C59R (n=3).
- No copy-number analysis (for example mdr1 amplification) was done.
